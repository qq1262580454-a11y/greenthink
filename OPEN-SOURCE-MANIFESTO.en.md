# Greenthink Open Source Manifesto

> **Your creativity deserves to be seen.**

---

## Prologue · The folder at 2 AM

At 2 AM, he closed the generator he had been tuning all evening, saved the final work into a folder named `FINAL_VERSION_never_edit_again_v7`, and shut down the computer.

The work was good. He knew it as he stared at the screen — it solved a real problem, carried months of his thinking, and was more complete than many paid products out there.

But no one would see it tomorrow. Or the day after. The folder would sit in his drive until it was swept away during some routine disk cleanup.

**This story happens almost every day.**

Countless people now have the power to create — yet there is still no channel for their work to *be seen*. We built cars but no roads. Greenthink wants to build that road.

## 一、Why we exist

For years, the loudest conversations in the AI community have been: which model is better, how to tune this prompt, what step count, should the seed be fixed.

Everyone is buried in "how to generate."

Parameter fanatics will tell you about their 40 LoRA iterations. Debugging warriors will recount three days stuck on one error. Prompt hoarders keep thousands of spell-like templates in their phones.

None of this is wrong. It is all part of creation.

But we noticed: while everyone discusses the *process*, **nobody seriously shows the *result*** — the complete, mature, finally-finished thing.

Someone finished it at 2 AM, closed the computer, and it vanished.

Greenthink exists to open those folders, put the work under the light, and say:

> Look. Someone made this. It is complete, it is mature, it is the final result. It deserves to be seen.

## 二、What we believe

Four sentences, each backed by real stories:

**Ideas over parameters.**
Parameters go stale, models get replaced, but a unique idea is still unique ten years later. We have watched too many people burn their passion chasing a "perfect parameter" and forget what they originally wanted to say. We remember ideas, not parameter tables.

**Creativity over technique.**
Technique can be learned, copied, surpassed. But creativity is yours — it comes from your experience, your field, your 2 AM spark. When technique and creativity conflict, we stand with creativity.

**Results over process.**
Process is private: your errors, your retries, your struggles belong to you. But results are public: they belong to everyone who might draw inspiration from them. We carry results, we do not spectate process.

**Imagination over tools.**
Tools grow stronger and cheaper until everyone has them. When tools stop being the barrier, the only difference between people is imagination. Greenthink bets on imagination.

## 三、Why open source

Honestly, one person could build Greenthink alone — the stack is simple, the code is a few dozen files.

One person can build a car.

**But only many people can build a road.**

A road that carries creations to more people needs someone to lay the gravel (code), someone to paint the signs (design), someone to drive the trucks (content), and someone to tell people far away the road exists (sharing). That is not a one-person project, and it should not be.

So Greenthink has been open from day one:

- Code is open — anyone can read, change, or take it
- Data is public — one JSON file, verifiable by anyone
- Direction is shared — major changes are discussed before they are made

We believe: **an open Greenthink maintained by many people will go further than any version a lone genius builds behind closed doors.**

## 四、Who we welcome

You might think "open source" is for programmers. It is not.

Greenthink welcomes you, whoever you are —

**If you are a creator:**
You may not know a line of code. But you made a complete work. Submit it. You do not lack talent — you lack a place to show it. We will build the stage.

**If you are a designer:**
You will see that the colors could breathe better, the layout could be airier. You have the eye the project needs.

**If you are a developer:**
The README is written, issue templates are ready, `good first issue` labels are hung. Pick a task, or open an issue for what you think should exist. Git history will keep your name forever.

**If you are just passing by:**
Even thinking "that idea is not bad" and forwarding it to a friend who is still building a car in the garage — you are already part of this road. Sharing is the lightest contribution.

**Whether or not you code, there is a place for you.**

## 五、Our promises

Promises are not slogans; they are rules. These five are written into our charter:

1. **Work always belongs to its creator.**
   The credit for what you submit is forever yours. The platform is a display case, not a collector. We never claim others' ideas, and they never become platform assets.

2. **Review stays neutral.**
   Review judges three things only: is it complete, is it mature, is it real. No taste judgment, no discrimination against schools — an illustrator and a network engineer's work receive the same respect here.

3. **The platform is free forever.**
   Greenthink does not charge creators, does not sell ad slots, and will not profit from content in the future. If servers ever need money, we will ask the community first — not touch the content.

4. **Data is transparent.**
   The work data is just a public JSON in this repo. Anyone can view, verify, or study it. We build trust on transparency, not promises.

5. **Open co-building.**
   Any major change affecting the community is discussed before execution. Your input will be taken seriously — even if it is not adopted, it gets an honest explanation.
